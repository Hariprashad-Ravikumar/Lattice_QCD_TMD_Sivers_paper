\section{Amplitudes at the remaining momenta}
\label{app:amps}
Section~\ref{sec:amplitudes_lattice} shows the extracted amplitudes $\tAmp_{2B}$ and $\tAmp_{12B}$ at the smallest simulated momentum, $\vect{P}\cdot a\hat{L}/(2\pi)=\{-1,0,0\}$ (Figs.~\ref{fig:A2B_3D_P1}--\ref{fig:A12B_eta8_P1}). This appendix gives the same four figures at the three larger momenta, $\vect{P}\cdot a\hat{L}/(2\pi)=\{-2,0,0\}$, $\{-3,0,0\}$ and $\{-4,0,0\}$, with $\hat\zeta=0.294367$, $0.539684$ and $0.785756$. In each case the amplitudes are plotted first in the discrete $(b_L/a,b_T/a,\eta|\vect{v}|/a)$ lattice grid and in the Lorentz-invariant $((\vect{P}\cdot\vect{b})\hat{L}/(2\pi),b^2/a^2,\eta|\vect{v}|/a)$ space, and then at fixed $\eta|\vect{v}|/a=8$.
